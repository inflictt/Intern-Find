How to Create an ATS-Friendly Resume

Complete Step-by-Step Guide using Overleaf (LaTeX)

Step 1: Tools Required

We will use Overleaf.com – a free online LaTeX editor.

✅ How to set up Overleaf:

  1. Go to overleaf.com
  2. Click on "Register" (top right corner).
  3. Sign up using your Google account or email.
  4. Once signed in, click on "New Project" > "Blank Project".
  5. Give your project a name like "Data Analyst Resume".

Overleaf autosaves your progress, and no software installation is required!

Step 2: Resume Structure

Your resume should have these 7 key sections:

1. Header 2. Summary 3. Skills 4. Experience 5. Projects 6. Awards 7. Education

Step 3: Section-by-Section Content

1️⃣ Header

Top section containing your name, designation, phone, email, and LinkedIn.

Shakra Shamim
Data Analyst
📞 +91-9919XXXXXX | ✉️ shakrashamim09@gmail.com | 🔗 linkedin.com/in/shakrashamim

2️⃣ Summary

Your elevator pitch in 2–3 lines. Summarize skills and goals.

Data Analyst with a strong foundation in SQL, Python, Excel, and Power BI. Skilled in cleaning, analyzing, and visualizing data to generate insights and support data-driven decision-making. Interested in collaborating on meaningful analytical work in fast-paced environments.

3️⃣ Skills

Group similar tools for readability.

Programming & Querying: SQL, Python
Data Visualization: Advanced MS Excel, Power BI, Tableau
Analytical Abilities: Data Cleaning, EDA, Insight Generation, Descriptive Statistics

4️⃣ Experience

Internships or freelance/remote roles. Focus on impact and quantification.

B.Sc. Information Technology

✅ Bonus Tips for ATS-Friendly Resume

  • No Tables/Columns/Images in LaTeX (our template handles this).
  • Use simple fonts and clear headings.
  • Use keywords from job descriptions (SQL, Data Cleaning, Power BI).
  • Use measurable metrics ("Increased efficiency by 30%").
  • Save as PDF (Overleaf → Download PDF).
  • Keep resume to 1 page only.
